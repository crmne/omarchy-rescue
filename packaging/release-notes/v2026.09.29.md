The first release of Omarchy Rescue: a USB stick that boots a live Omarchy console with the rescue tools you'd reach for from SystemRescue, and Claude Code, Codex, and OpenCode ready to help you diagnose and fix a machine that won't boot. No desktop needed: it all runs on a proper console with Omarchy's fonts and colors.

**Download** `omarchy-rescue-2026.09.29-x86_64.iso` below, check it with `sha256sum -c omarchy-rescue-2026.09.29-x86_64.iso.sha256`, and write it to a USB stick.

![The Omarchy Rescue welcome screen](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/crmne/omarchy-rescue/v2026.09.29/docs/screenshots/welcome.png)

## New

- **AI agents on a rescue stick.** Claude Code, Codex, and OpenCode are installed and know where they are: how an Omarchy install is laid out (LUKS, Btrfs subvolumes, Limine, snapper), how to read its journal and chroot into it, and to diagnose read-only first and never run anything destructive without a clear yes. By @crmne.
- **Your install, mounted in one command.** `omarchy-rescue-mount` unlocks an encrypted Omarchy disk and mounts the whole system at `/mnt` from its own fstab, ready for `arch-chroot /mnt`. `--unmount` puts it all away again. By @crmne.

  ![omarchy-rescue-mount unlocking and mounting an encrypted install](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/crmne/omarchy-rescue/v2026.09.29/docs/screenshots/mount.png)

- **A real console without a desktop.** kmscon on tty1 with JetBrains Mono, truecolor, and the Tokyo Night palette, inside Omarchy's tmux and shell setup. A basic console boot entry covers graphics cards kmscon can't drive. By @crmne.
- **Sign in from your phone.** `omarchy-rescue-login` starts each agent's browserless sign-in; `C-Space u` shows the last link on screen as a QR code, and `omarchy-rescue-share` puts the whole console in your phone's browser so you can paste sign-in codes back. By @crmne.
- **The rescue toolbox.** Everything in Arch's live ISO (ddrescue, testdisk and photorec, smartmontools, nvme-cli, partclone, fsarchiver, clonezilla, and more) plus chntpw, sbctl, limine, os-prober, memtester, stress-ng, lshw, hwinfo, rclone, restic, borg, and network diagnosis tools. pacman points at the online repos, so anything else is one install away. By @crmne.

## Known limitations

- Claude Code's sign-in link is too long for a QR code on smaller screens; use `omarchy-rescue-share` to finish on your phone.
- `omarchy-rescue-share` serves a root shell over plain HTTP on the local network, behind a random address. Stop it with `omarchy-rescue-share stop` when done.
- The console keyboard layout is US.
- Tested in QEMU virtual machines, not yet on real hardware. x86_64 only.
